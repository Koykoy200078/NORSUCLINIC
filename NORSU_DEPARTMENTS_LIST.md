## Database Implementation

These departments are now implemented in the NORSUCLINIC system:

-   **Table:** `departments`
-   **Seeder:** `DepartmentSeeder.php`
-   **Model:** `App\Models\Department`
-   **Used In:** Patient registration form for Faculty position selection
-   **Relationships:**
    -   Each department belongs to a college (`college_id`)
    -   Each department can have multiple users (faculty members)

---

## Field Mapping in Patient Form

### Faculty Employee Selection:

-   **Shows:** Department dropdown (pulls from departments table)
-   **Saves to:** `department_id` field in users table
-   **Hides:** Campus, College, Course, Office fields

### Staff Employee Selection:

-   **Shows:** College dropdown + Office dropdown
-   **Saves to:** `college_id` and `office_id` fields
-   **Hides:** Campus, Course, Department fields

### Student Selection:

-   **Shows:** Campus, College, Course, Year Level dropdowns
-   **Saves to:** `campus_id`, `college_id`, `course_id`, `year_level_id` fields
-   **Hides:** Position, Department, Office fields

### Guest Selection:

-   **Shows:** None (all academic fields hidden)
-   **Saves to:** `year_level_id = 8` (Guest)
-   **Hides:** All academic fields

## Notes

1. **Department-College Relationship:** Each department is linked to its parent college through the `college_id` field
2. **Faculty Assignments:** Faculty members will select their department from this list when registering as patients
3. **Data Integrity:** The department list is based on actual NORSU course offerings as of 2025
4. **Future Updates:** If NORSU adds new programs or departments, update the `DepartmentSeeder.php` file accordingly
